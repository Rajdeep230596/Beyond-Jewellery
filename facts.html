How it is grown

Weeks, not millennia

A sliver of diamond seed goes into a chamber. Carbon-rich gas is broken down with extreme heat, and layer by layer the carbon settles onto the seed in the same cubic lattice a mined diamond forms in. The rough is then cut, polished and graded by the same people, on the same scales.

Step 01

The seed

A thin diamond slice is placed in a growth chamber — the blueprint every new carbon atom follows.

Step 02

The growth

CVD or HPHT conditions break carbon out of gas at thousands of degrees. The crystal thickens over weeks.

Step 03

The cut

Our cutters shape it into one of twelve forms — including the half moons and kites nobody asked us for.

Step 04

The certificate

Graded for the 4Cs, given a unique SKU ID, and paired with BIS/HUID hallmarked gold before it ships.

Care & cleaning

Keep the fire alive

◆

Warm water, soft brush

A few drops of mild soap and a baby toothbrush once a month lifts the film that dulls sparkle. Rinse, pat dry.

◆

Last on, first off

Put jewellery on after perfume and lotion, take it off before the gym, the pool and the kitchen sink.

◆

Store separately

Diamond scratches diamond. Keep each piece in its own pouch rather than tumbling loose in a box.
